The platform

A biofoundry produces whole-organism data. The interactome structures that data and maps it onto human biology, and XenoEx reasons over the result to decide what to run next.

Run

The automated biofoundry

Transparent zebrafish larvae share 82% of human disease-associated genes and carry a working liver, heart, nervous system and blood-brain barrier. ZebraChip microfluidics load each larva and hold it in a fixed lateral position; FishEye computer vision reads morphology and behaviour. We built the pipeline for the New Approach Methodology framework of the FDA Modernization Act 2.0.

Structure

The Multi-Species Systemic Interactome

The interactome is our data asset: continuous morphology, behavioural matrices and transcriptomics, co-registered over several days on the same individuals, in cohorts large enough to show population variability. We add client in vitro and organoid data and external omics, then map the result onto human biology through cross-species orthology and clinical priors.

Reason

XenoEx, interpretable multi-agent AI

XenoEx reasons over the interactome in steps a scientist can read, audit and redirect. It humanises signatures, scores candidates for systemic efficacy and multi-organ toxicity, re-evaluates shelved assets and ranks target hypotheses. When its confidence falls under the client's agreed threshold, it designs the validation run and sends it to the biofoundry.
