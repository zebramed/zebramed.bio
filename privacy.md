## What the website collects

Nothing, by itself. zebramed.bio is a static website: it sets no cookies, uses no audience measurement, advertising or social-media trackers, stores nothing on your device and loads no third-party resources. Fonts, scripts, images and videos are all served from zebramed.bio. With no cookies or similar trackers, there is nothing to consent to, which is why the website has no cookie banner.

Links to LinkedIn take you to LinkedIn's own website, which has its own privacy policy.

## Our host's server logs

Like any web server, the one that delivers this website receives your IP address and the technical details of each request (the page requested, its date and time, your browser). Our host, {{ c.host.name }} ({{ c.host.service }}), logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub may process them in the United States. It is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission recognised as providing adequate protection on 10 July 2023, and also relies on the Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## When you email us

When you write to us, for example with the "Start a validation pilot" or "Contact investor relations" buttons (which open your own email software), we process the data you choose to send: your name, email address, organisation and role, the content of your message and any attachments.

- **Purpose:** to answer you and, where relevant, to discuss and follow up a pilot, partnership, investment or other business relationship.
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in answering the people who contact us and in developing our business relationships (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), or steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- **Is it required?** No. Writing to us is voluntary; without your contact details we simply cannot reply.
- **Who receives it:** the members of the ZebraMed team concerned, and our email service providers, which act as processors on our behalf: Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany), which hosts our mail server, and Microsoft (Microsoft 365). Where Microsoft processes data outside the European Union, the transfer is covered by the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the Commission's standard contractual clauses. We do not sell your data or pass it on for marketing.
- **How long we keep it:** up to three years after our last exchange if no contract follows. If a contract follows, for its duration and then for the legal limitation period of five years; documents we must keep for accounting purposes are kept for ten years.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling.

## Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the French Data Protection Act (loi Informatique et Libertés), you can ask to access, rectify or erase your data, restrict its processing, object to it and, where applicable, receive it in a portable format. You can also give instructions on what should happen to your data after your death. Write to [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}); we answer within one month.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL, 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France ([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr)), or with the data protection authority of the EU country where you live or work.

## Security

The website is served only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Access to the emails you send us is limited to the people who need them, and our providers apply security measures appropriate to the risks.

## Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if the way we handle personal data changes, for example if the website starts collecting new data. The date at the top of the page shows the current version.
