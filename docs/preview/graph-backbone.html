Platform · how it works

The Systemic Translation Graph, animated

A systemic backbone, fed by whole-organism data, optionally joined by other models, and read out as human risk profiles that rank candidates. Four renderings of the same storyboard, switched in the bar above; the story either plays by itself (Auto) or follows the page as you scroll (Scroll), with or without a subtle camera. "Film" is the directed version, exportable to video. The clips need the page served over HTTP, not opened from disk.

Illustrative. Clips from ZebraMed imaging and tracking; the heatmap is computed from simulated data; candidates and values are fictional.

What it shows

Systemic backbone

Organs and the systems linking them, with molecular signals and functions around each organ. It is labelled as the map, not as a species: the figure never says "zebrafish".

Whole organism

One input among others: imaged (a lateral clip: raw, vessels, then segmentation) connects to organs, tracked (one well, a swim bout at half speed) to functions, sequenced (a clustered heatmap) to molecular signals.

Other models

Each attaches where it can observe: cell cultures at molecular signals, organoids at single organs (liver, brain), an organ-on-chip coupling two organs (gut and liver). Each branch crosses the backbone to reach its place; its inset docks on the side.

Human risk profile

Predicted human risk by organ for four candidates, then ranked by their highest organ risk, safest first.

Experiment clips and heatmap

  • Imaged: phenotype1.mp4, cropped 4:1 around the larva; raw, vessels, then brain and spinal cord into full segmentation, skipping the transitions. 9.6 s loop.
  • Tracked: behaviour2.mp4, well E6 only, labels cropped out, colour reduced so it doesn't read as the violet; one bout with rest either side, at half speed. 8.8 s loop.
  • Sequenced: 36 genes × 12 samples of simulated dose-response data (vehicle, low, mid, high), z-scored and ordered by hierarchical clustering; the bar above marks dose.

Cuts are in docs/preview/media/make_clips.py; edit the windows there and run it again. Swap the heatmap for a real one when there is a dataset to show.

Film and video

"Film" runs the same story on a directed camera: in close on the backbone, over to each input as it arrives, along each model's branch, back for the whole system, then along the data to the risk profile. The story slows where there is something to read. 16:9 ends on the whole picture; 4:5 ends on the ranking.

Export: python3 docs/preview/film/export.py renders every frame in headless Chromium and writes MP4, WebM and a poster for both formats to docs/preview/film/.

Open points

  • The violet for other models is new to the palette.
  • Six organs shown; immune system, skin or blood vessels could be added.
  • Ranking by worst organ is one choice; a weighted score is another.
